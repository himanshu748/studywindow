BEFORE YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

A few good questions.

For everything else, the original course page is your next stop.

Which courses can I explore?+

StudyWindow currently supports the public Cambridge Professional and Continuing Education catalogue. It is an independent local tool and is not affiliated with the University of Cambridge.

Does a match mean I can enrol?+

A match applies to a specific published fact, such as the listed Home fee fitting your budget. Admission, fee-status eligibility, complete schedules, and a place on the course require confirmation with the provider.

Are the examples live?+

The interactive landing preview and “recorded example” use genuine saved retrievals with their original timestamps. In the workspace, explicit search, inspection, comparison, and health actions make new TinyFish calls when live access is enabled.

Can I use it from my AI assistant?+

The local MCP server exposes typed search, inspection, comparison, and health tools. Installation and host configuration are included in the project README. The web workspace calls the same MCP server.
