## Product and meaningful endpoints

StudyWindow helps working learners explore Cambridge PACE courses and compare tuition, application windows and published commitments with their practical limits. Each dated offering keeps its own fees and deadline. Matches, conflicts and missing information include source links and retrieval times. Online delivery never establishes schedule compatibility.

TinyFish Agent operates the catalogue form and delivery filter. Fetch retrieves fresh page evidence. Isolated Browser sessions open the course panels and read literal fields. Comparison is deterministic after fresh retrieval of every course; health checks exercise the supported source contracts. These are three meaningful endpoint families under the observed scoring schedule; acceptance and points remain organizer decisions.
