# Course-discovery product study

Observed 4 November 2026. Primary official public pages only; no sign-in, enrolment, paid calls, or external writes. This was a rendered-page text/control study through the web tool. Filter controls, tabs, and list actions were visible, but their client-side transitions, persistence, hover styling, and animation were **not** exercised. The Class Central search URL was inaccessible to the web tool; its public Psychology category supplied the catalogue view. Maven's “Starting soon” link was followed and returned the matching URL, heading, and reordered catalogue.

## Recommended original direction

Build two distinct surfaces: a focused landing page that demonstrates the decision mechanism, then a compact course workspace. Retain StudyWindow's window motif and restrained green/ink identity, but replace the static oversized illustration with a small interactive study-window preview. A budget slider, availability controls, and course selection should visibly change evidence-backed match/conflict/unknown states. Label recorded evidence with its actual date and never present the preview as a fresh search.

The workspace should emphasize a search/filter bar, scannable result cards, a persistent shortlist, and an inspection drawer or focused detail panel. Put detailed receipts behind disclosure. A comparison matrix or offering tabs should prevent the current repeated finding cards from becoming a very long reading task.

Motion is a proposed StudyWindow treatment, **not** a copied or observed competitor animation: short card entrances, a clear selection-to-shortlist transition, progress feedback during real requests, and an animated transition between discovery and comparison. Avoid decorative loops; use the user's motion skill, keyboard controls, and reduced-motion alternatives. No competitor branding, copy, statistics, ratings, testimonials, or course imagery should be transplanted.

## Rendered Shortcoursesportal interaction

The parent also opened [Shortcoursesportal search](https://www.shortcoursesportal.com/search/course) in the in-app browser, expanded Tuition Fee, entered a maximum, and observed the selected-filter chip, updated URL, loading placeholders, and filtered cards. The mobile screen kept programme type, delivery, duration, fee basis and a wishlist action close to each title. StudyWindow uses that information hierarchy and clear interaction feedback; it does not offer a fee search filter before inspecting source fees.

## Implemented visual and motion direction

Two real local routes: `/` for the product landing page and `/app` for the course workspace. Ink, pine, lime and light neutral surfaces replace the old continuous editorial page. Manrope headings and DM Sans body text are served locally; original font licenses are included in `demo/assets`. The landing preview reads genuine saved evidence and makes no paid calls. Budget and weekday controls, keyboard tabs, FAQ disclosures and links are functional. Unknown teaching commitments remain unknown.

Motion uses a consistent `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)` curve: immediate press feedback, roughly 240 ms content changes and about 500 ms once-only section entrances. Motion communicates selection, navigation and result changes. There are no autoplay decorative loops or fake provider progress. Reduced motion removes spatial animation. The workspace keeps loaded-page sorting distinct from catalogue search, and recorded results distinct from live retrieval.

Primary font sources: [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans), [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope). No competitor branding, testimonials, counts or imagery are reused. Final rendered verification is recorded separately.
